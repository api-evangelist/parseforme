# ParseForMe

A document-parsing / intelligent-document-processing service that converts PDFs, scans and photos (invoices, bank statements, receipts, resumes, purchase orders, shipping docs, utility bills, payslips) into typed, confidence-scored fields, exportable to JSON/CSV/XLSX/Google Sheets or driven via REST.
